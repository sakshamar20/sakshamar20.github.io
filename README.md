# Saksham Arora

I am a graduate student pursuing a joint master's in Statistics and Computer Science at Purdue University. My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning, causal inference, quantitative finance, experimentation, and data-driven decision-making.

Before Purdue, I worked at Flipkart as a Business Analyst and Principal Homepage Analyst, developing experimentation and causal-inference frameworks, personalization systems, and real-time machine-learning pipelines. I previously completed a Mitacs Globalink research internship at the University of British Columbia and earned my B.Tech. in Chemical Engineering from IIT Kanpur, with minors in Machine Learning and Computer Systems.

My current research explores bilevel reinforcement learning for circular manufacturing. Other projects include quantitative trading strategies, knowledge graphs for education, post-wildfire debris-flow prediction, and CUDA-based particle simulation.

Beyond data and research, I am a photographer and former coordinator of the Photography Club at IIT Kanpur. I have also passed CFA Level I.
